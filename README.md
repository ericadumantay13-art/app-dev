# My Favorite Series

## Title: The Summer I Turned Pretty

**Genre:** Romance, Drama

**Author:** Jenny Han

**Main Characters:** Belly Conklin, Conrad Fisher, and Jeremiah Fisher

### About the Series
The Summer I Turned Pretty is a romantic drama series about a girl named Belly who spends her summers at Cousins Beach. Her life becomes complicated as she experiences love, friendship, family problems, and growing up.

### Why I Like It
I like this series because it has a beautiful love story, interesting characters, and emotional moments. I also enjoy the summer setting and the relationship between the characters.

### My Favorite Quote
> Everything good, everything magical happens between the months of June and August.
